# #145 — public route walk

The capture ran on 2026-09-27 (`out/2026-09-27T22-01-51`): v2 = `main` at `df8a239`, v3 = `migration/chakra-v3` at `5f1c709`. It covered 32 public routes, in light and dark mode, on both versions. Screenshots show v2 on the left and v3 on the right. Rule: **broken on both** means environment or pre-existing; **v3 only** means regression. Nothing here has been fixed.

An environment note: seeded images are absolute URLs, so they load on both versions. The missing `CDN_HOST` only affects images uploaded through the app. The empty map on `/event/:id` is identical on both.
