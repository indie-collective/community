# #146 — authenticated flows and forms

Run: 2026-09-28, `flows.mjs` against v2 = `main` at `df8a239` and v3 = `migration/chakra-v3` at `5f1c709`, plus the render captures from #145's run. Flows sign in as `harness-member` or `harness-admin` (#166), act through the real UI, and check the database where the flow should change it. Records a flow creates are removed afterwards. The per-flow table is in [`146/flows-table.md`](146/flows-table.md). Nothing here has been fixed.

## Works on both

- Signing in by form, and the resulting session (`/profile` → 200).
- Logout clears the session (`/profile` → 302 afterwards).
- Client-side validation fires on `signin`, `signup`, `forgot` and the create forms (v2):
  - Empty required fields are blocked by the browser's native `required` bubble.
  - An invalid email is blocked natively.
  - A signup password mismatch shows "Passwords must match" through `Field.ErrorText` on v3, so the `FormErrorMessage` → `Field.ErrorText` swap works where it's wired. No account is created.
- Username availability: `/check-username-availability` answers `{"available":false|true}`, and the form shows ✓/✗ (icon only, see A9).
- Org delete through Options → Delete → confirm dialog: row deleted, redirect to `/orgs`.
- `/admin/tags` add and delete; `/admin/changes` and `/admin/missing` render.
- `event.$id/games.delete` removes the link when posted directly on both versions. On v3 its button is unreachable (A3).

## Broken on both — pre-existing

- **The profile can't be saved while Last name or About is empty.** yup rejects the `null` value (`about must be a \`string\` type, but the final value was: \`null\``). Any account without those fields is stuck until both are filled. Not a regression, but worth a ticket.

## Unverifiable locally

- **`signup` submission:** `pgcrypto` is absent locally.
- **`forgot` → `reset.$token`:** needs `SENDGRID_API_KEY` and `BASE_URL`.
- **Image upload** (`profile.edit` avatar, `game.$id/images.add`, org logo, event cover): needs S3 credentials. It's also blocked by A4 on v3.
- **`games.create` IGDB lookup:** needs `IGDB_*`. It's also blocked by A1 on v3.
- **Discord OAuth** (`auth/$provider`, callback).
- **Toast rendering** (A6) can't be observed until A1 is fixed.
